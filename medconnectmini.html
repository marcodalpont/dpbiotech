DP Mini Companion App

Your pocket-sized
Surgical Command Center.

Med Connect Mini unlocks the full potential of your smartphone's sensor. It's your monitor, your recorder, and your sterile controller.

Professional Portfolio

Your Medical Legacy

Manage your career highlights in one secure location. Edit your surgical recordings, upload new cases, and curate your professional profile. Transform your raw clinical data into a dynamic digital CV that showcases your expertise to peers, institutions, and patients worldwide.

  • 👤
    Granular Privacy

    Complete control over your data. Decide exactly which cases are broadcast to the community and which remain encrypted private records for personal review.

  • ✏️
    Case Management

    A searchable archive of your work. Add ICD-10 codes, patient notes, procedural tags, and pre-op/post-op imaging to organize your surgical history.

  • 📊
    Impact Analytics

    Measure your influence. Track views, saves, and engagement stats to see how your techniques are helping the global surgical community.

The Community

Connect & Discover

Med Connect Mini connects you to a global network of surgical excellence. Stay on the cutting edge by accessing a curated feed of live surgeries, technique breakdowns, and clinical pearls from key opinion leaders worldwide.

  • 🔔
    Smart Notifications

    Never miss a learning opportunity. Set customizable alerts to know the moment a mentor goes live or when a new case matching your specialty interests is published.

  • 🌍
    Global Search Engine

    Find the exact reference you need. Filter the global feed by pathology or specific surgical technique to prepare for your next complex case.

  • 💬
    Peer Discussion

    Engage in high-level clinical debate. Comment on specific video timestamps, ask technical questions, and verify techniques in a secure, professional environment.

Bring your own AI Architecture

Supercharged Intelligence

We don't just give you tools; we give you a platform. Med Connect Mini is designed to be model-agnostic, allowing you to connect your personal or enterprise AI accounts (OpenAI, Google Cloud, Anthropic) directly into the surgical workflow via secure API keys.

Whether you prefer the reasoning of ChatGPT or the multimodal capabilities of Google's Gemini, you can harness them to automate the administrative burden of surgery. Turn voice dictation into structured reports or analyze case data instantly.

  • 📝
    Automated Op-Notes

    The app listens to your intra-operative voice notes and uses your connected AI to format them into perfect, billing-ready surgical reports automatically.

  • 🔍
    Semantic Case Search

    Forget keywords. Ask your library: "Show me the last three times I treated a Class II furcation defect," and let the AI find the exact timestamps.

Universal Streaming

Broadcast Anywhere.

You don't always need the full OR setup. Go live directly from your phone to share lectures, rounds, or quick consultations.

📱 Pocket Mode

Stream using your phone's native camera. While you won't get the DP Mini's stereoscopic 3D view, you get the full power of our low-latency broadcasting network.

  • 👀
    Watch Live

Computational Imaging

Smart Filtration

Go beyond standard optics. The app leverages the GPU to apply real-time Chroma and Luma keying, allowing you to isolate specific tissues or suppress background distractions instantly.

  • 🎨
    Chroma Key (Color Isolation)

    Isolate specific color spectrums (e.g., hemoglobin red) to emphasize vascularity or highlight pathological markers while desaturating surrounding tissue.

  • 💡
    Luma Key (Brightness Filter)

    Filter based on luminance to visualize depth and texture. Perfect for distinguishing nerve fibers or enhancing visibility in deep cavities.
