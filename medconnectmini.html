Computational Imaging

Smart Filtration

Go beyond standard optics. The app leverages the GPU to apply real-time Chroma and Luma keying, allowing you to isolate specific tissues or suppress background distractions instantly.

  • 🎨
    Chroma Key (Color Isolation)

    Isolate specific color spectrums (e.g., hemoglobin red) to emphasize vascularity or highlight pathological markers while desaturating surrounding tissue.

  • 💡
    Luma Key (Brightness Filter)

    Filter based on luminance to visualize depth and texture. Perfect for distinguishing nerve fibers or enhancing visibility in deep cavities.
