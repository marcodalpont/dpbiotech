What's Next

01 — In Development

Six pillars, quietly engineered.

Detailed specs stay under wraps until launch — but the design philosophy doesn't. Below are the six principles guiding the DP Pro's architecture. Each is being verified now in our Treviso lab; full datasheet, certifications and pricing land at launch.

Pillar 01

Decoupled stability

An optical body that floats independently from the eyepieces — the image stays still while you reposition.

Pillar 02

Effortless reach

A counter-balanced articulated arm that locks exactly where you leave it — no drift, no fatigue across long procedures.

Pillar 03

Intelligent context

An optional, non-diagnostic visual layer that keeps surgical context — the surgeon stays in command, always.

Pillar 04

Hands-free control

Sterile-field-safe voice and pedal control on the suite computer — no cloud round-trip, no fingerprints.

Pillar 05

Documentation built-in

Capture, annotate and trim cases without leaving the OR — every procedure becomes a teachable moment.

Pillar 06

Connected suite

Streaming, recording and telementoring built directly into the OR — Med Connect Pro from day one.
