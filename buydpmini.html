    Regulatory information

    CEMDR Class INon-sterileMR Non-Safe

    Medical device

    DP Mini is a CE-marked active Class I medical device under Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR), self-certified by the manufacturer under Art. 19 MDR. Expected service life: 10 years.

    Intended purpose

    Magnified visualization of the surgical field and anatomical structures during medical and surgical procedures. It is a visual aid only: it does not perform diagnosis, measurement or therapy.

    Intended users

    Qualified healthcare professionals only: surgeons, medical specialists and trained OR technicians. Read the warnings and the instructions for use carefully before use.

    Contraindications

    Not for MRI environments, nor in the presence of flammable anesthetic mixtures, concentrated oxygen or nitrous oxide. Supplied non-sterile and cannot be sterilized: near the surgical field, cover it with a certified single-use sterile drape.
