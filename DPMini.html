Designed & Made
01Variable-resistance mode

Optics travel proportional to handle force.

The optical head answers to the handle like an extension of your hand. A light push moves it just enough to reframe; a firmer one sweeps it through its whole range. There is no mode to switch and no knob to loosen: how hard you push decides how far it goes.

  • Fine framingMicro-corrections with a fingertip, without losing the field.
  • Full sweepReposition the head in one continuous gesture.
  • One gripFraming and repositioning from the same handle.
02Pressure-sensing release

Minimal force unlocks the joint. Let go and it re-locks.

The joint senses the pressure of your hand. Push and it releases, so the whole microscope swings freely; let go and it locks again exactly where you left it. No brake levers, no drift, no second hand needed to hold it in place.

  • Light-touch releaseMinimal force is enough to unlock the joint.
  • Instant re-lockLet go and the position is held.
  • One-handedReposition mid-procedure while the other hand keeps working.
03Ergonomic eyepiece positioning

Set your working posture without moving the optics.

The eyepieces ride on their own articulated arm, separate from the optical head. Raise them, lower them or bring them closer to match how you sit or stand, while the optics, and your field of view, stay exactly where they are.

  • DecoupledEyepiece height and reach change; the field of view doesn't.
  • Always levelThe eyepieces keep their attitude through the whole movement.
  • Seated or standingAdapt the microscope to your posture, not the other way round.
