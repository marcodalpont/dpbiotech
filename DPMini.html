Intended Use

Built for the field. Used everywhere.

DP Mini is engineered for clinical environments where precision, mobility and documentation matter. Its modular configuration adapts to specialty-specific working distances and workflows.

Dentistry
Restorative, endodontic and prosthetic procedures with hands-free magnification.
Ophthalmology
Anterior segment exams and minor procedures with sharp depth perception.
Dermatology
Lesion inspection, dermoscopy capture and follow-up documentation in 3D.
ENT
Otoscopy, in-office endoscopy support and outpatient minor surgery.
Pathology & Lab
Specimen review and case-sharing with peers via Med Connect streaming.
Veterinary
Mobile examination across small-animal and equine settings.
Education & Training
Live classroom streaming with stereoscopic 3D for surgical curricula.
Field & Outreach
Portable clinical missions where weight and setup time are critical.
